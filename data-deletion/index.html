DATA DELETION INSTRUCTIONS

Effective Date: June 7, 2026 | Last Updated: June 7, 2026 | Version: 1.0

Entity: Project Baseline Inc.

Project Baseline Inc. ("Project Baseline," "PB," "we," "our," or "us") operates a Meta (Facebook and Instagram) connected application used to manage advertising and content for Project Baseline and the properties we operate. This page explains what data that application handles and how you can request deletion of any data associated with you.

If you have used Facebook or Instagram to interact with one of our pages, ads, or tools and you want any associated data deleted, email todd@project-baseline.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We complete verified requests within 30 days.

1. What our Meta application does

Our connected application is an internal advertising and content-operations tool. We use it to create and manage advertising campaigns, publish and schedule content, and review aggregated performance reporting across the Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) for Project Baseline and the properties we operate.

The application authenticates against business accounts that we own or are authorized to manage. It is not a consumer login product: we do not offer "Log in with Facebook" sign-up, and we do not build advertising or marketing profiles of individual end users from Meta data.

2. What data is involved

Through the Meta platforms, our application may access:

  • Business and advertising data for accounts we own or manage: campaign settings, ad creative, budgets, and aggregated, de-identified performance metrics (impressions, clicks, reach).
  • Page and content data for pages we operate: posts, scheduled content, and aggregated engagement counts.
  • Public interaction data that Meta associates with a page or ad, such as a public comment you leave on a post, handled in accordance with Meta's own platform terms.

We do not collect or store the contents of your private Facebook or Instagram profile, your friends list, your private messages, or your password. Payment, billing, and personal data we collect through our own websites and services are governed by our Privacy Policy.

3. How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete any data associated with you that our Meta application holds. Two options:

Option A: Email us (recommended)

  1. Send an email to todd@project-baseline.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."
  2. Tell us how you interacted with us (for example, the Facebook or Instagram page, the ad, or the tool) and the name, handle, or email address associated with you so we can locate any records.
  3. We acknowledge your request within 7 calendar days and complete it within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address or account of record to verify your identity before we delete anything.

Option B: Remove it yourself on Meta

Some data lives on Meta's platform under your control. You can remove a public comment or interaction directly on the post, and you can review or remove our application's access to your information at any time in your Meta account settings:

  • Facebook: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and Websites

Removing the application's access on Meta stops further data access. To also delete records we already hold, use Option A.

4. What we delete and what we may keep

When we complete a verified deletion request, we delete the personal data we hold that is associated with you, except where we are required to retain certain records by law (for example, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention records, or aggregated and de-identified metrics that can no longer be linked to you). If we must retain anything, we will tell you what and why.

5. Your broader privacy rights

This page covers data tied to our Meta connected application. For your full set of privacy rights, including access, correction, export, and deletion of personal information we hold through our own websites and services (and rights under CCPA, GDPR, and other laws), see our Privacy Policy or email todd@project-baseline.com.

Submit a data deletion request

Email todd@project-baseline.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We respond within 7 days and complete verified requests within 30 days.
